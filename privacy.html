🔒 Privacy Policy

LogiXcube - 3D Rubik's Cube Trainer
Short version: LogiXcube does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything works locally on your device. No registration, no tracking, no ads.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how LogiXcube ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile and web application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

By using LogiXcube, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

LogiXcube is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID)
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other personal data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

LogiXcube stores the following information only on your device using browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Game progress — current cube state, move history
  • Local leaderboard — your best solving results (move counts, times)
  • Settings — language preference, sound on/off, theme
  • PRO status — subscription status (if activated)

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Opening the leaderboard (tap the logo on the cube) → "Clear History"
  • Clearing your browser data / app data
  • Uninstalling the App

4. In-App Purchases (PRO)

LogiXcube offers an optional PRO subscription. All payments are processed through trusted third-party platforms:

Google Play Billing (Android)

When you purchase PRO on Android, the transaction is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google's Privacy Policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy

Apple In-App Purchase (iOS)

When you purchase PRO on iOS, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's Privacy Policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy

Important: We only receive a confirmation that a valid purchase was made. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details from Google or Apple.

5. Third-Party Services

LogiXcube includes links to our social media profiles. Clicking these links opens external websites with their own privacy policies:

  • Facebook — facebook.com/privacy
  • Instagram — privacycenter.instagram.com
  • YouTube — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Telegram — telegram.org/privacy
  • TikTok — tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • X (Twitter) — x.com/en/privacy

LogiXcube does not embed tracking pixels, cookies, or SDKs from these platforms.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

LogiXcube is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

Parents and guardians can be confident that their children's privacy is fully protected when using LogiXcube.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. However, you have full control over locally stored data:

  • Right to access — all data is on your device, accessible via browser settings
  • Right to deletion — clear app data or uninstall the App
  • Right to data portability — not applicable (no data collected)
  • Right to object — not applicable (no data processing)

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to external servers, the security of your data depends on your device's security. We recommend:

  • Using a screen lock (PIN, password, or biometric)
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Not sharing your device with untrusted users

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
